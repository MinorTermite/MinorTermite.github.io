How I understand life

I talk about what I have come to through years of my own search. Your choice stays with you.

A person and the body

For me a person is a soul, life itself. The body is the form through which we live our experience. That is why knowing yourself is linked both to your inner life and to how you treat your body.

The natural state

I know and I say it plainly: a person does not need food. You can live on food, but you pay for it. I call life without consuming food the natural state and connect it with spiritual growth. In my reflections I also speak about life without water.

How long a person can live

I believe a person can do far more than we usually think. Living with consumption, a person can reach a hundred and fifty or two hundred years. Without consumption you can live three hundred, five hundred, a thousand years and leave on your own, when you decide.

The capacity of the brain

Talking about what a person is capable of, I compared 1–2% with 5–7% and reflected on the full opening of the brain. This is how I expressed how much in us is still unopened.

Spiritual growth

I connect a person's growth with the soul and the Creator. For me it covers the whole of life: thoughts, actions, habits and how you treat people.

The body as a conductor of light

When a person lives in the natural state, the body becomes a conductor of light and energy. The light of the Creator passes through it. Such a body easily draws energy, and with it everything needed in material life.

On the path and the body

Briefly about what I have learned on myself. Open what resonates.

Food as a dependency

I do not divide food into right and wrong. For me any food keeps a person dependent, only with different strength. Heavy food holds you the hardest, fruit less, juice even less, then water. After that comes the way out into the natural state. A person can eat and enjoy it, that is their right. But they pay for it.

Why people say it does not suit them

When a person does not like something, the mind quickly finds arguments against it. This is how any habit protects itself. More often these words hide an unwillingness to go through changes in the body, in mood, in appearance. Cold, weakness, breaking habits. It is hard, I know it from myself.

Everyone at their own level

Some take short breaks from food, some have become vegetarian or raw food eaters. I once went through veganism myself. I do not judge anyone. Everyone stands on their own step and will move further in their own time, if they practise.

Why the beginning is hard

At the start of the natural state you will lose to any athlete. There can be weakness, sluggishness, a heavy head. Those who have knowledge or support keep going. Today three kilometres, tomorrow four, then five. Slowly, but better every day.

Why nothing happens fast

The human body is part of nature, and nothing in nature happens fast. A tree does not grow to the sky overnight. In the first, second and third year changes keep coming. Many leave the path right here: they lack the patience to wait it out.

How the body renews itself

When a person stops consuming, the body no longer spends its strength on taking food in and getting it out. All systems renew, cells are gradually replaced, the internal organs switch on fully. A person grows younger.

Possibilities without limit

Everything around us is built like a fractal. If you can run ten kilometres, you can run twenty, fifty, a hundred, a thousand. A lot depends on training, but just as much on the state you do it in, with consumption or without it.

What the body does at night

By evening the body, loaded with food all day, switches off. It needs sleep, because at night it sorts out everything a person has thrown into it. That is why late in the evening there is almost nobody outside. At that time I go for a calm run.

How food leaves the body

Fruit passes easily. Fried potatoes, meat, pasta or a bar of chocolate take months to leave. So there is no need to eat three times a day. It is enough to have a dense meal of whatever you like once every three days.

Why teeth decay

I tested on myself what makes teeth decay. The decay starts with food cooked on fire. Dairy, cheese, eggs, fish and meat add to it.

Tomatoes

If you still eat fruit and vegetables, take a closer look at tomatoes. They have less sugar than fruit, and they carry the red colour that matters for the body. Eat them as they are, without salt and pepper.
