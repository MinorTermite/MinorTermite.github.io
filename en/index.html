How I understand life

I talk about what I have come to through years of my own search. Your choice stays with you.

A person and the body

For me a person is a soul, life itself. The body is the form through which we live our experience. That is why knowing yourself is linked both to your inner life and to how you treat your body.

The natural state

I know and I say it plainly: a person does not need food. You can live on food, but you pay for it. The natural state is life without food and without water. I connect it with spiritual growth.

How long a person can live

I know a person can do far more than we usually think. Living with consumption, a person can reach a hundred and fifty or two hundred years. Without consumption you can live three hundred, five hundred, a thousand years and leave on your own, when you decide.

The capacity of the brain

Talking about what a person is capable of, I compared 1–2% with 5–7% and spoke about the full opening of the brain. This is how I show how much in us is still unopened.

Spiritual growth

I connect a person's growth with the soul and the Creator. For me it covers the whole of life: thoughts, actions, habits and how you treat people.

The body as a conductor of light

When a person lives in the natural state, the body becomes a conductor of light and energy. The light of the Creator passes through it. Such a body easily draws energy, and with it everything needed in material life.

On the path and the body

Briefly about what I have learned on myself. Open what resonates.

Food as a dependency

I do not divide food into right and wrong. For me any food keeps a person dependent, only with different strength. Heavy food holds you the hardest, especially killed animals. It pins a person to the ground at once. That includes everything people call animal products: they even came up with a formula for it and hung a label on it. In fact it is dead flesh. We become what we eat. If we eat what is dead, we become dead ourselves. An animal gives milk only to its own young, just as a mother gives milk to her child. Next comes what is killed, boiled, fried, baked: grains, porridge, cooked vegetables. That is dead food too. Fruit holds you less, juice even less. After that comes the way out into the natural state. A person can eat, enjoy it and relieve stress with it. That is their right, and they must not be judged for it. But they pay for it.

Why people say it does not suit them

When a person does not like something, the mind easily finds arguments against it. This is how the mind protects itself. More often these words hide an unwillingness to go through transformation: changes tied to the rebuilding of the body, working on yourself physically and psychologically, changes in appearance. Cold, weakness, breaking habits. It is hard, I know it from myself.

Everyone at their own level

Some take short breaks from food, some have become vegetarian, some raw food eaters, some fruitarians. I once went through all these paths myself: vegetarianism, veganism, raw food, fruit only, juice only. I went up and down all these food chains many times. I do not judge anyone. Everyone stands on their own step and will move further in their own time, when they are ready.

Practice matters more than theory

Practitioners change the world. Those who went through everything themselves rather than studied it from the side. I spent thirty years of my life on this, and for fourteen of them I studied my body every day. I came to the very edge of life and death. That is why I only speak about what I have tested myself.

Why the beginning is hard

When you step onto the path of knowing yourself and enter the natural state, the people around you are used to seeing you differently. But changes start to happen in your face, in your body, in everything, and it frightens you too. Especially if you are an athlete: at the start of the natural state you will lose to any athlete. Physical results drop because the body is rebuilding. There can be weakness, sluggishness, a heavy head. Many tell themselves: better eat, sleep, and everything will be fine. That is a choice too. Those who have knowledge or support keep going. Today three kilometres, tomorrow four, then five. Slowly, but easier every day.

Why nothing happens fast

The human body is part of nature, and nature is alive, nothing in it happens fast. A sapling does not become a big tree overnight. Hair and nails do not grow fast either: everything takes time. It is the same with the body in the natural state. In the first, second and third year changes keep coming. Many leave the path right here: they lack the patience to wait it out.

How the body renews itself

When a person stops consuming, the body no longer spends its strength on taking food in and getting it out. All systems renew, cells are gradually replaced, and a new form of life builds up in the same body. The internal organs switch on fully. A person grows younger. People my age grow old, while I grow younger with the years.

Possibilities without limit

Everything around us is built like a fractal. If you can walk a kilometre, you can walk ten. If you can walk ten, you can walk a hundred. If you can walk a hundred, you can walk a thousand. It is the same with running: if you can run ten kilometres, you can run twenty, fifty, a hundred, a thousand. A lot depends on training, but just as much on the state you do it in, with consumption or without it.

What the body does at night

By evening the body, loaded with food all day, switches off. It needs sleep, because at night it sorts out everything a person has thrown into it. That is why late in the evening there is almost nobody outside. At that time I go for a calm run.

How food leaves the body

Fruit passes easily. Meat, fried potatoes, pasta, chocolate and all this rubbish take months to leave. So for those who still stay in consumption and do not go further, out of fear or by their own choice, I recommend eating only fruit, moving more and eating no more than once or twice a day.

Why teeth decay

I tested on myself what makes teeth decay. Teeth decay most of all from any food cooked on fire. Dairy, cheese, eggs, fish and meat belong here too.

Tomatoes

If you still eat fruit and vegetables, take a closer look at tomatoes. They have less sugar than fruit, and they carry the red colour that matters for the body. Eat them as they are, without salt and pepper.
